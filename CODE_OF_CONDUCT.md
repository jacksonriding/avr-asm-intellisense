# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making this project welcoming and respectful for everyone.

- Be respectful in all interactions.
- Keep discussions focused on technical and project-quality outcomes.
- Assume good intent and provide constructive feedback.
- Keep reports detailed and actionable.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on code, docs, and behavior, not personalities.
- Accept constructive feedback and respond promptly when action is required.
- Prioritise user safety and security in decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project infrastructure (spam, abuse, sabotage).
- Any behavior that makes collaboration unsafe.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, block participants, or restrict contribution
privileges for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private issue or direct message
to project maintainers via the repository channels.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from common OSS community practice for open-source projects.
